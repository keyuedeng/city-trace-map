About this map

This map documents a field survey conducted on 28 August 2026, tracing a route from Southern Cross Station to Parkville, Melbourne, and recording the surveillance and tracking infrastructure encountered along the way — part of a university assignment on planning a route that minimises the digital trace left behind while moving through the city.

How to use this map
  • Hover or tap a coloured pin for details — what it is, who operates it, what data it collects, and a photo.
  • The route line's colour shows the transport mode used for that segment (see the legend below).
  • 🚩 and 🏁 mark where the route begins and ends.
  • Use the point list below to jump straight to any location, grouped by technology type.
  • Collapse this sidebar with the ◂ arrow for a full-screen view.
